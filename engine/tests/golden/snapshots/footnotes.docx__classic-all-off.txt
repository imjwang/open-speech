# Field Notes on Cupping (docx, 12 blocks) · classic
H Field Notes on Cupping
¶ We cupped twelve lots from three origins over two days. Each lot was scored by four tasters1 and the scores were averaged.
¶ 1 Scores follow the 2004 protocol with a 100-point scale.
H Method
¶ Samples were roasted light and rested for a day. Brewing water was heated to 93 °C2. The full protocol is described at https://example.net/cupping.
¶ 2 Water was filtered to 120 ppm total dissolved solids.
¶ Grind size was fixed for every sample.
¶ Cups were covered until the break.
H Results
¶ The washed lots scored highest overall, as the table shows.
¶ Origin, Mean score. Ethiopia, 87.5. Colombia, 85.0.
¶ Clarity beats intensity when the cups cool.
