# Field Notes on Cupping (docx, 12 blocks) · classic
H Field Notes on Cupping
¶ We cupped twelve lots from three origins over two days. Each lot was scored by four tasters and the scores were averaged.
H Method
¶ Samples were roasted light and rested for a day. Brewing water was heated to 93 °C. The full protocol is described at.
¶ Grind size was fixed for every sample.
¶ Cups were covered until the break.
H Results
¶ The washed lots scored highest overall, as the table shows.
¶ Clarity beats intensity when the cups cool.
