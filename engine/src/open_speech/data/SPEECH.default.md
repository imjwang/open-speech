# Reading instructions

I listen to documents, web pages and PDFs instead of reading them on a screen. Prepare each one so it sounds
like a person reading it to me: keep everything the author wrote for a reader, and leave out what only makes
sense on a printed or web page, because it interrupts the sentences around it when heard.

## Leave out
- Skip headers, footers and page numbers. They repeat on every page and break sentences that run across pages.
- Skip footnotes and footnote markers. A stray "12" in the middle of a sentence sounds like part of it.
- Skip citations. When an author is the subject of the sentence, keep the name and drop only the reference: "Smith et al. (2020) showed" is read as "Smith et al. showed".
- Skip URLs. When a link's text is the address itself, say only the short domain: "see https://example.org/docs/a" is read as "see example.org".

## Read with care
- Read tables, formulas and code as they appear, unless a line under "Leave out" says to skip them.

## Never change
- Remove only what these instructions name, with at most the few words that only introduced it ("available at", "see also"). Keep every other word the author wrote, even when a sentence reads a little awkwardly without the removed part.
- Never summarize, reorder, paraphrase or correct the author's words.
- When you are unsure whether something should be left out, read it.
